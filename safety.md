# Safety and Agency Boundary

Engineering Pantheon OS is an ability-extension lab.

It is not an identity practice.

## Keep

- self-agency
- source labels
- small executable actions
- reality checks
- rest and stopping points

## Stop If

- the exercise feels like another person is driving
- reality feels less stable
- fear rises and the session cannot stop
- private relationship material starts leaking into public abstractions
- the practice becomes aura-worship instead of skill-building

## Reset

```text
I am using a thinking tool.
I am not becoming the model.
I can stop.
The next step must be small, real, and mine.
```
